# About this Godaan reading edition

Premchand's complete Hindi novel is presented as selectable text in 36 chapters. Swipe left or right to change chapters. Long press near the center of the reading area for chapters, reading settings, bookmarks, highlights, and notes.

The previously bundled 611-page PDF is archived in the project as `content/godaan-original-611-pages-hi.pdf`. Its embedded Hindi text layer contains many character-recognition errors. An earlier app transcription also contained obvious errors, including “मालम” for “मालूम”. This corrected reading copy uses [Hindi Wikisource's page transcriptions of गोदान.pdf](https://hi.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A0:%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8.pdf/%E0%A5%A7%E0%A5%A7), pages 11–328. Of the 318 source pages, 308 were marked validated and 10 proofread when prepared. The text was rendered from the transcribed pages, split at all 36 chapter headings, and checked for complete chapter and page coverage. It is a different edition of the same complete novel, so it does not preserve the archived PDF's page breaks or exact wording.
