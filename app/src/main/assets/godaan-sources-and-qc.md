# About this Godaan reading edition

Premchand's complete Hindi novel is presented as selectable text in 36 chapters. Swipe left or right to change chapters. Long press near the center of the reading area for chapters, reading settings, bookmarks, highlights, and notes.

The previously bundled 611-page PDF is archived in the project as `content/godaan-original-611-pages-hi.pdf`. Its embedded Hindi text layer contains many character-recognition errors, so the app uses the complete chapter transcription at [Hindi Wikisource](https://hi.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%8B-%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8) for readable text. The 36 chapter pages are [listed here](https://hi.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A3%E0%A5%80:%E0%A4%97%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8). This transcription is from a different edition of the same complete novel, so its chapter text does not preserve the PDF's page breaks or wording exactly.
