# *Godaan*, Chapters 6–7: language and transcription verification

## Scope and sources

The Reader copies at `app/src/main/assets/godaan-chapters/06.txt` and `07.txt` were checked paragraph by paragraph against the Wikisource facsimile/transcription of *Premchand Rachanavali–6*, *Godaan*, Chapters 6 and 7. The primary chapter pages are [Chapter 6](https://hi.wikisource.org/wiki/प्रेमचंद_रचनावली_६/गोदान-६) and [Chapter 7](https://hi.wikisource.org/wiki/प्रेमचंद_रचनावली_६/गोदान-७); the corresponding facsimile pages are printed pp. 53–73 and 73–97. I also consulted the separate older scan/transcription [गो-दान/६](https://hi.wikisource.org/wiki/गो-दान/६) and [गो-दान/७](https://hi.wikisource.org/wiki/गो-दान/७) where the Rachanavali scan itself appeared grammatically or typographically doubtful. The second scan is used as a parallel witness, not silently treated as the same edition.

Line wrapping was normalized, but paragraph boundaries, quotation marks, dandas, word division, diacritics, and dialogue dashes were checked. Chapter 6 contains 219 numbered paragraphs in the supplied file; Chapter 7 contains 373. Chapter 7’s stray Chapter 8 heading and opening matter on the Wikisource transclusion were excluded because the supplied file ends at the Chapter 7 conclusion. The table uses the supplied paragraph numbers. `[new paragraph]` and `[paragraph end]` are editorial markers used only to make layout discrepancies explicit; they are not part of the novel.

A visible `|` in an existing-text cell represents the supplied file’s vertical-bar substitute for a danda. Rows marked `Retain` are deliberate non-corrections: dialect, historical vocabulary, an edition’s printed grammar, or a source variant. Rows marked `Source-level` identify an apparent error already present in the Rachanavali print; they should not be described as an OCR error in the Reader file.
